Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Hafeez ur Rehman ("we", "us", or "our") operates the CAGF — Claude AI Game Factory mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our App.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

a. Advertising Data

The App uses third-party advertising services, including Google AdMob. These services may collect and use the following information:

  • Device Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • Device model, OS version, and language settings
  • IP address (used for approximate location and fraud prevention)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)
  • App usage data related to ad delivery

This data is collected by Google AdMob and is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

b. In-App Purchase Data

The App offers optional in-app purchases (cosmetic items, family pass, ad removal). Purchase transactions are handled entirely by Google Play Billing / Amazon Appstore. We do not collect or store your payment information (credit card numbers, bank details, etc.).

c. App Usage Data

The App may store the following data locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences:

  • Game progress (coins, gems, levels reached)
  • Cosmetic preferences (owned skins, equipped skin)
  • Purchase status (ad removal, family pass)

This data is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information solely to:

  • Serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements
  • Improve the App's performance and user experience
  • Process in-app purchases
  • Prevent fraud and ensure compliance with platform policies

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

The App is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. The App is tagged as "Designed for Families" on Google Play where applicable.

If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete such information promptly.

The App uses non-personalized ads and does not use behavioral profiling for children.

5. Data Retention

  • Advertising data: Retained by Google AdMob according to Google's retention policy.
  • App data (coins, skins, etc.): Stored locally on your device only. Deleted when you uninstall the App.

6. Data Security

We are committed to protecting your information. The App does not transmit personal data to our own servers. All advertising and payment processing is handled by trusted third-party services (Google) with industry-standard security measures.
